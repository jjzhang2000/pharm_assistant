# Little Medicine Box User Manual

Little Medicine Box is a small tool that helps you keep track of "how much medicine is left at home and when to restock". It estimates the remaining supply based on the schedule you enter, and reminds you before you run out.

---

## 1. Main screen

After opening Little Medicine Box, swipe left or right to switch pages. From left to right:

- **First page: Shortage page** (shown by default when you open the app). All medicines that are running low are gathered here, so you can see at a glance "which medicines are almost out".
- **Each following page: a medicine box**. Each box contains the medicines you have added.

**The top title bar** changes with the page:
- On the shortage page, it shows "Shortage".
- On a box page, it shows the box name.

**The small dots at the bottom** indicate which box you are on; the current box's dot is longer and brighter.

> Tip: Besides swiping, you can also drag the top title bar left or right to quickly flip pages.

---

## 2. Adding a medicine

On any box page, tap the round **＋** button in the bottom-right corner to add one. The form has three parts:

### 1. Medicine info
- **Medicine name** (required).
- **Dose**: enter a number, then choose the unit and form (e.g. "500 mg / tablet").

### 2. Schedule
- **Every X days/weeks/months, N times**: e.g. "2 times a day", "once a week".
- **Per dose**: how much each time.
- **Dose time**:
  - Choose "day" and give specific times (e.g. 08:00, 20:00).
  - Choose "week/month" and give the specific date and time.

### 3. Inventory
- **Current stock**: how much you have now.
- **Remind when below X days**: remind you when the remaining days fall below this number. The default is 7 days. You can change it as needed.

Tap **Add** at the bottom to save.

---

## 3. Editing and deleting a medicine

**Edit**: tap a medicine card to open its details, then tap the **pencil icon** in the top-right corner.

**Delete**: in the details page, tap "More" (the three-dot icon) in the top-right → **Delete**, then confirm.

---

## 4. Quickly adjusting stock

Two ways:

1. **On the medicine card**: tap the **－／＋** buttons below the card to decrease or increase stock by 1.
2. **In the details page**: there are **－1、＋1** buttons plus two quick-add buttons **＋28、＋100** (changeable to **＋10、＋50** in Settings), handy for restocking a whole box or using up a whole bottle quickly.

The remaining days update immediately after adjusting.

---

## 5. Starred medicines

Stars mark the medicines you care most about (for example, ones that must be taken on time).

- Tap the **star** on a medicine card to star or unstar it (a solid red star means starred).
- Starred medicines are **placed at the front of the box list**, and grouped at the top of the shortage page.
- Starred medicines trigger a phone notification when they fall below the reminder threshold (see "Reminders" below).

---

## 6. Managing boxes

In the "More" (three-dot) menu at the top-right, you can:

- **New box**: create a box.
- **Rename**: change the box name.
- **Delete**: delete the box; all medicines inside are deleted too.

**Moving a medicine to another box** — two ways:

1. **Long-press and drag**: long-press a medicine card on a box page; the screen turns into box thumbnails. Drag the card onto the target box and release. Dragging it onto "New box" creates a new box and moves it in.
2. **Move from the details page**: in the details page, "More" → **Move**, then choose a target box or create a new one.

---

## 7. Backup box

The app has a fixed **Backup box**, always last among all boxes.

It stores medicines you "bought on a whim but have not started yet". Features:

- It only records quantity, **does not auto-deduct**, and does not participate in shortage reminders.
- Good for cold medicine, external-use medicine, and other as-needed items.

---

## 8. Shortage page

This page helps you quickly see medicines that "need restocking":

- **Top**: all starred medicines that are running low are grouped together.
- **Below**: grouped by box, listing the ordinary medicines that are running low in each box.
- Tapping a box group jumps directly to that box.

There are three buttons in the top-right corner:

- **Share icon**: saves the whole shortage list as a long image to your photo album, handy for sending to family or a nearby pharmacy.
- **Gear icon**: opens Settings.
- **Info icon**: opens the Info page with version info, user guide, and version history.

If there are no shortage medicines for now, this page shows "No shortage medicines".

---

## 9. Reminders

Little Medicine Box reminds you to restock in two situations:

1. **Low stock**: when a starred medicine's remaining days fall below its "reminder threshold".
2. **Almost out**: when any medicine (including non-starred) is about to run out.

**Not receiving reminders?** Check whether the system allows "Little Medicine Box" notifications:

- Go to **Settings → Apps → Little Medicine Box → Notifications** on your phone and turn on notifications.

> Note: only starred medicines alert when "below threshold"; non-starred medicines alert only when "about to run out".

---

## 10. Settings

How to open: the **gear icon** in the top-right of the shortage page.

Here you can:

- **Theme**: choose "System" / "Light" / "Dark" and one of four accent colors (Teal / Blue / Green / Purple).
- **Quick add stock**: choose the two quick-add button increments in the details page (+10/+50 or +28/+100).
- **Language**: 中文 / English, takes effect immediately.

---

## 11. FAQ

**Q: How are the remaining days calculated?**
A: Estimated automatically from the "current stock" and "schedule" you entered. The supply is deducted step by step according to your frequency (daily / weekly / monthly) to estimate roughly how many days are left.

**Q: In what order are the cards in a box arranged?**
A: Starred medicines come first; the rest are sorted by "remaining days" in ascending order, so the ones running out soonest are at the top.

**Q: Why did I not get a low-stock reminder?**
A: First confirm that "Little Medicine Box" notifications are enabled in system settings. Second, only **starred** medicines alert when below threshold; ordinary medicines alert only when "about to run out".

**Q: Do medicines in the Backup box trigger reminders?**
A: No. The Backup box only records quantity; it does not auto-deduct and does not participate in shortage reminders.
