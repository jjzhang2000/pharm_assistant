隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026-10-29

感谢您使用「小药箱」（以下简称「本应用」）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本政策旨在向您说明本应用如何处理您的信息。请在使用前仔细阅读。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传您的任何个人身份信息。

本应用是一款本地药品库存管理工具，核心功能完全运行在您的设备上。您录入的数据——包括药品名称、规格、剂量、服用频次、库存数量、星标等——仅保存在您设备本地的存储文件中，不会以任何形式传输到我们的服务器或任何第三方。

2. 信息的使用方式

您录入的药品信息仅用于在本设备上实现以下功能：

  • 计算药品剩余天数与库存扣减；
  • 按服用安排生成用药提醒通知；
  • 展示药品的短缺状态。

所有计算与处理均在设备本地完成，我们不接触、不访问这部分数据。

3. 信息的存储

  • 您的数据以本地文件的形式保存在您的设备上；
  • 本应用不需要联网即可正常使用；
  • 卸载本应用将删除设备上的本地数据（如已开启系统备份，则遵循系统自身的备份策略）。

4. 权限使用说明

为实现特定功能，本应用会申请以下系统权限，均仅用于功能本身，不涉及任何数据收集：

  • 通知权限（Android 13 及以上）：用于在药品短缺或用药时间到达时向您发送本地通知提醒；
  • 开机自启动（可选）：用于设备重启后重新安排提醒，确保提醒不丢失；
  • 存储读取（仅 Android 9 及更早版本）：用于保存分享图片到相册。

5. 第三方服务与数据共享

  • 本应用不含任何广告、埋点统计或分析 SDK；
  • 本应用不接入任何第三方服务，不共享、不出售、不披露您的任何信息。

6. 数据安全

由于您的数据完全保存在本地设备中，数据安全由您的设备系统与您自身的保管方式决定。我们建议您妥善保管设备，避免安装来源不明的应用。我们无法访问您设备上的数据，也不会因任何原因向您索取这些数据。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下的儿童，也不会收集儿童的个人信息。因本应用不收集任何个人信息，故不存在相应的收集行为。

8. 政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们将在本应用内或本页面更新并注明新的生效日期。请您定期查阅。

9. 免责声明

本应用仅用于个人药品库存与提醒管理，不构成任何医疗建议。任何用药决策请咨询专业医师或药师。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Thank you for using "Little Medicine Box" (the "App"). We value your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your information. Please read it carefully before use.

1. Information We Collect

This App does not collect, store, or upload any of your personal information.

The App is a local medicine inventory management tool whose core features run entirely on your device. The data you enter — including medicine name, specification, dosage, dosing frequency, inventory quantity, and starred status — is stored only in local files on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

2. How We Use Information

Entered medicine information is used solely on your device to:

  • Calculate remaining days and deduct inventory;
  • Generate medication reminder notifications according to your dosing schedule;
  • Display shortage status.

All calculations and processing occur locally on the device; we do not access this data.

3. Data Storage

  • Your data is saved as local files on your device;
  • The App works fully without an internet connection;
  • Uninstalling the App deletes the local data on your device (subject to your system backup settings).

4. Permissions

To support specific features, the App may request the following system permissions, used solely for those features and not for any data collection:

  • Notifications (Android 13+): to send local reminder notifications when a medicine runs low or a dose is due;
  • Boot receiver (optional): to reschedule reminders after a device restart so they are not missed;
  • Storage access (Android 9 and earlier only): to save shared images to the gallery.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

  • The App contains no ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs;
  • The App integrates with no third-party services and does not share, sell, or disclose your information to anyone.

6. Data Security

Because your data is stored entirely on your device, its security depends on your device system and how you safeguard it. We recommend keeping your device secure and avoiding apps from unknown sources. We cannot access the data on your device and will never ask you for it.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. As the App collects no personal information, no such collection occurs.

8. Policy Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it within the App or on this page and note the new effective date. Please review it periodically.

9. Disclaimer

The App is intended only for personal medicine inventory and reminder management and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified physician or pharmacist for any medication decisions.
